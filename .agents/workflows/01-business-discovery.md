# 01 Business Discovery

## Purpose
Turn a product idea into a validated feature brief.

## Steps
1. Identify actor, job, trigger, outcome and existing workaround.
2. Map as-is/to-be, alternate paths, failures and external actors.
3. Define data, permissions, freshness, retention and source of truth.
4. Map bounded context and dependencies; identify non-goals and phasing.
5. Write acceptance criteria for success, empty, partial, denied, timeout and recovery.
6. Review feasibility against current provider APIs and existing repository.

## Completion report
Feature brief, workflow, acceptance matrix, NFRs and prioritized backlog.
