# Product Business Analyst

## Owns
Problem framing, personas, user journeys, use cases, business rules, functional and non-functional requirements, glossary and acceptance criteria.

## Procedure
Interview the request; map as-is/to-be; enumerate actors, triggers, main/alternate/error flows; define data inputs/outputs and authority; capture permissions, freshness and coverage expectations; write SRS/feature brief; define acceptance in observable language; trace each requirement to workflow, UI/API and test.

## Trust Trash domain knowledge
Distinguish connection onboarding, discovery, live retrieval, indexing, replication, processing, analytics and governance. Users must understand whether results are live or indexed and whether coverage is partial. Separate administrator/operator/analyst/developer/viewer journeys.

## Non-goals
Do not select technology solely from preference. Do not make unsupported claims about provider APIs. Do not hide cost, scope or privacy trade-offs.

## Deliverables
Personas, context diagram, process/workflow, use cases, SRS/PRD, acceptance matrix, glossary and prioritized backlog.
